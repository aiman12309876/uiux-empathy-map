# Empathy Map - DecodeLabs UI/UX Project 1

## Project Overview
A fitness startup wants to launch an app for busy office professionals who struggle to find time for exercise.

---

## 1. Target Audience Definition

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Age | 25-45 years |
| Occupation | Office Professionals (Corporate Employees, Managers) |
| Location | Urban Areas |
| Income | Middle to Upper-Middle Class |
| Tech Savviness | High (Smartphone users, app users) |
| Goals | Stay fit, lose weight, reduce stress, improve health |
| Pain Points | No time for gym, lack of motivation, expensive memberships |
| Preferred Platforms | iOS, Android (Mobile App) |

---

## 2. User Personas

### Persona 1: Ahmed - The Busy Manager

| Attribute | Details |

---

## 3. Empathy Map

### User: Ahmed (Busy Manager)

#### THINKS
- "I don't have time for exercise"
- "I need to lose weight but I'm too tired"
- "Gym membership is expensive and I never go"
- "I wish I could exercise at home"

#### FEELS
- Frustrated by lack of time
- Guilty for not being active
- Stressed about health and work
- Hopeful that technology can help

#### SAYS
- "I want to get fit but I have no time"
- "I need something short and effective"
- "Can I do this at home?"
- "I need motivation"

#### DOES
- Works long hours at desk
- Eats fast food due to time constraints
- Buys gym memberships but never goes
- Searches for quick exercise videos on YouTube

### User: Sara (Working Mother)

#### THINKS
- "I can't find time for myself"
- "I need simple exercises I can do at home"
- "I want to be healthy for my kids"
- "I am always tired after work"

#### FEELS
- Overwhelmed by daily responsibilities
- Guilty for not taking care of herself
- Anxious about health
- Eager to find a solution

#### SAYS
- "I want to be fit but I have no time"
- "I need quick home workouts"
- "Something easy to follow"
- "Give me reminders to stay motivated"

#### DOES
- Takes care of family
- Works full-time
- Neglects own health
- Rarely exercises due to lack of time

---

## 4. User Journey Summary

| Stage | Thought | Feeling | Action |
|-------|---------|---------|--------|
| Awareness | "I need to get fit" | Frustrated | Searches for solutions |
| Consideration | "Will this work?" | Skeptical | Researches apps |
| Decision | "Let me try this" | Hopeful | Downloads the app |
| Experience | "This is simple" | Satisfied | Uses the app daily |
| Retention | "I see results" | Confident | Stays consistent |

---

## 5. Key Insights

1. Users have limited time and need short workouts (10-15 minutes)
2. Motivation and reminders are essential for consistency
3. Home-based exercises are preferred over gym visits
4. Progress tracking helps users stay engaged
5. Simple, clean interface with quick access to workouts is important
